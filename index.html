AI at Work

AI at Work (@aiatworkbiz) is a YouTube short-video channel that teaches office workers and small business owners how to use AI tools to get real work done faster.

The "AI at Work" application is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos and read the channel's own analytics.
