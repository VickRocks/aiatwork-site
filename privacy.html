Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy describes how the "AI at Work" application ("the App") handles data. The App is operated by the owner of the YouTube channel AI at Work (@aiatworkbiz).

What the App does

The App is used solely by the channel owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's own analytics and statistics. It is not offered to or used by anyone else.

Data we collect

The App does not collect, store, or process personal data of any other users. The only data accessed is the channel owner's own YouTube channel data, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, with the owner's explicit consent.

Storage of tokens

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are never uploaded to any server operated by us or anyone else.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data obtained through Google APIs to any third party.

Data retention and deletion

Statistics about the channel owner's own channel, read through the YouTube API, are stored only in a local file on the channel owner's computer and are used only for the owner's own performance reports. OAuth tokens are stored in a private folder on the same computer. No data is stored on any server.

When the app is no longer used, or if access is revoked at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, the tokens and the locally stored statistics are deleted. The channel owner can delete this data at any time; deletion requests can also be sent to the contact email below.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
